## Abstract

We tested the effect of soil pH on the germination rate and early growth of radish (*Raphanus sativus*) seeds over a 14-day period. Five groups of 20 seeds were planted in soil adjusted to pH 4.5, 5.5, 6.5, 7.5, and 8.5 respectively, using agricultural lime and elemental sulphur as amendments. Germination peaked at pH 6.5 (85%) and pH 7.5 (80%), with sharply reduced rates at both extremes. Early root growth followed the same pattern, suggesting radish optimum lies between slightly acidic and neutral conditions. Our results are consistent with published agricultural guidance for Brassicaceae crops.
